How do I receive my purchase? ▾

Right after checkout, a download link is emailed to you. eBooks arrive as PDF. Apps (like Budget Tracker, HDB Mortgage Calculator) come with a personal unlock link. Sessions get a Calendly + Zoom invite.

Can I pay with PayNow? ▾

Yes. Singapore PayNow is supported alongside Visa, Mastercard, and Amex. Stripe handles the checkout securely.

What if I’m not happy? ▾

14-day no-questions refund on every product. Reply to your purchase email and I’ll process it within a day.

Is this financial advice? ▾

No. These are educational resources written by a CFP, but they’re not personalised advice. For licensed advice tailored to your situation, book the 1:1 Financial Simulation or contact me directly.

Is this Singapore-specific? ▾

Yes. Every product is built for Singapore residents — CPF rules, HDB loan structures, MediShield Life, SGX listings, MAS regulations. The frameworks travel, but the numbers and references are SG-first.

What if I already have a financial advisor? ▾

Use these as a second opinion. The eBooks and Policy Decoder are designed to make your existing meetings sharper — you’ll know what to ask, what numbers to push back on, and where commissions might be steering recommendations.

Can I expense this? ▾

If your employer reimburses professional development, yes — the receipt includes a clear product description and tax line. SkillsFuture credits aren’t supported (these are digital, not accredited courses).
